Questions

Honest answers.

What does Autows actually automate?

Repetitive work: replying to customers, capturing and following up leads, booking, moving data between tools, documents and reports.

What is an AI agent?

Software that can understand a request, decide what to do, use your tools and take action, handing over to a person when it is unsure.

Can Autows work with my existing tools?

Usually yes. We connect messaging apps, email, spreadsheets, calendars, CRMs and websites, often through n8n. We confirm compatibility in the first conversation.

Do I need technical knowledge?

No. Describe the work in plain language and we handle the setup and testing.

Can you automate WhatsApp or Instagram?

Yes, that is a core service. Platform rules and approvals apply, and we explain them upfront.

Can you build custom AI agents?

Yes. Agents are designed around your workflow, data and the decisions they may make.

Do you work with small businesses?

Yes. Small businesses, startups, agencies, restaurants, e-commerce and individuals are all a good fit.

How long does a project take?

It depends on scope. A focused automation can be far quicker than a multi-system agent. We give a realistic estimate after discovery.

How much does automation cost?

It depends on complexity and the tools involved. We scope first and quote clearly, with no surprises.

Can you maintain the system after launch?

Yes. Systems can be monitored and improved as your business changes, and we can agree support after launch.
